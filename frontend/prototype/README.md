# Lumen app screen

The working screen of Lumen for the manager of DaVita Cameron Park Dialysis. It turns a PG&E Public Safety Power Shutoff notice into a patient-by-patient treatment plan that staff approve. Static files, no build step. Deploy the folder to AWS Amplify as is. Serve it locally on port 5173, for example `python3 -m http.server 5173 -d frontend/prototype`, and open http://localhost:5173/ (not 127.0.0.1: the map key works only on the allowed referrers).

## What the screen does

* **Top bar.** The home clinic, the county select (one county at a time: El Dorado live, Santa Clara illustrative tour), the countdown chip ("Power off in 33 h · Simulated notice"), the theme toggle, the demo key, Reset and Run demo. When a plan exists, Run demo first asks "Start over?", because a new run deletes the patient plan, its approvals and its voice notes.
* **Left rail ("Shutoff response").** Eight steps in plain words: Read the PG&E notice, Find who is affected, Put the most urgent first, Find open chairs, Write patient messages, Check the plan, Approve the plan, Send voice notes. The line under the title says "Step 4 of 8 · Find open chairs", "Waiting for you · Approve the plan" or "Done". Each step shows one result line ("Cameron Park loses power Tue 6 PM to Thu noon", "40 patients have a treatment then", "28 move · 8 come in early · 4 need a call"). Nothing else: no source, model or service labels and no timings anywhere on the screen. Check the plan runs `POST /check` in the background as soon as the plan arrives, and again after each re-plan and approval; its result line reads "Nothing to fix · 2 to look at". The re-plan box sends a plain-language instruction (`POST /replan`; the API reads it with the model and code applies it) and adds a "Change: …" step whose result starts "Read as: …". Suggestion buttons: "DaVita Folsom can only take 4 more" and "Maria Delgado declined". A failed step turns red, shows the API `error` and has a Retry button.
* **The guided run (Run demo).** One hands-free run of about 30 to 45 seconds, with Pause and Resume in the rail header. The timeline at the bottom of the map is its clock, with a caption per scene: 1 "Notice received" (Mon 9:00 AM; `POST /alert`; a notice card slides in over the map), 2 "Shutoff area" (the clock runs to Tue 6:00 PM and the area goes dark; `POST /plan` runs meanwhile), 3 the camera flies into the home clinic in 3D, it loses power, then the camera pulls back as its patients appear, 4 "Searching nearby clinics, nearest first": the camera pulls back and `LumenMap.searchGraph` visits the open clinics and then the hospital in order of road time from each closed clinic (straight-line order until every road route is in; the tour's clinics one after another, about 10 s in all), each node showing "+16 patients", "Full" or "Not needed" ("Each patient gets the nearest clinic with a free chair at their time."); then the road routes the plan uses trace from the closed clinic to each receiving clinic (about 10 s at most); each clinic's chair count on the map and in the panel counts up from 0 as its route lands, 5 messages, 6 the run waits for staff ("Waiting for your approval"; the "Review and approve" button pulses three times). The approval list never opens by itself: the panel button or the rail's Approve step opens it. Camera moves in the run take 0.75 of the map's usual time. After approval, "Send voice notes" in the list selects every patient who is ready for a note. Nothing is sent until staff approve. Under reduced motion every scene is instant, in the same order. Reset returns to the powered home view with no notice and no timeline.
* **Map (center).** The shutoff area, live and de-energized feeders, transmission lines, the closed clinic ("No power", "8 come in early before 6 PM"), the receiving clinics ("16 of 18 chairs" with a fill bar, or "Full"), the hospital ("Hospital · 4 staff calls"), the affected patients (small dots at home) and one road route from the closed clinic to each destination (staff calls dashed). Selecting a patient (map dot, plan row or review row) shows only that patient's road route and a callout (`LumenMap.isolate`); a Chairs row in the panel, or a route on the map, shows only that route. Whole plan, Esc, closing the row or closing the review list after picking a row there shows every route again. Red means power off only. Controls: Whole plan (after the plan), Home and 3D. The legend is closed by default and lists only what the map shows now; it has a Pause animation button for the moving feeder current.
* **Road routes (Amazon Location Routes v2).** Computed live in the browser with `mapKey`: during the run for each closed clinic to destination pair the plan uses, after a re-plan for new pairs, and for each moved patient (home to new clinic) in the background after the routes are traced, so the approval list's Drive and Leave home columns fill in; a patient someone opens goes first. At most 4 requests at once, one retry on 429 or 5xx, cached in memory for the page session only. A failed route is a dashed straight line labeled "Road route not available". There are no precomputed route files.
* **Right panel.** Before a plan: the notice and the home clinic. With a plan: "Patient plan" and its state ("Needs your approval", "Approved", "N changed"), the counts, the big "Review and approve 40 patients" button (then "Every patient is approved" with a "Send voice notes" next step), "Chairs" (per receiving clinic: chairs this plan uses of the chairs open in the shutoff window from `GET /state` `clinics[].slots`, a fill bar, "Full", road time from the closed clinic; then how many patients need a staff call), and the patient rows. An open row is one card: a big sentence ("Moves to DaVita Folsom · Wed Oct 28, 6:00 AM"), the leave line from the plan, the same time the message gives ("Leave home by 5:28 AM · 21 min drive · 15.1 mi"; the miles come from the road route), "Why first: …", "Long gap after this session" when flagged, the message (collapsed, "Message · Spanish, English") and Mark confirmed (after approval), Decline and Play voice note (after approval). In the tour the voice button calls `POST /speak` with the demo key: a template translation is read as is (`translated: true`); a language with no template is translated first (labeled "Machine translation").
* **Review and approve (`review.js`).** The way to approve: a list over almost the whole screen with filters, search, a sortable table, Approve selected and Approve all, and the same patient card per row. The panel button (and the rail's Approve step while it waits) opens it; it never opens by itself. When every row is approved it shows "Every patient is approved" and the counts.
* **Check the plan card (panel).** Above the Review button: "Check the plan · 1 to fix · 5 to look at". Blocks one per line ("Fix: …"), warnings one line per kind ("32 patients · No hepatitis B test date on file"), and "Call instead: …" for patients who live alone with a pickup in the dark or have heart failure and a long gap. Patient IDs in check sentences become names; 24-hour times become "5:27 AM".
* **Review list (`review.js`).** Five columns: select box, Patient (language under the name), Change (where and when under it, and one flag line when the check flags something that changes what staff do), Leave home, Status. Filters: All, Needs approval, Ready to send, Sent, Call these. A header box selects every visible row; the bulk bar at the bottom has Approve (k), Send voice notes (m) and Clear. Send voice notes first runs a dry run (`POST /notify` with `dry_run`) and shows who gets a note and who is skipped and why (already has this note, needs a staff call, the plan check says to call instead, not approved yet, keeps the usual session, has a problem to fix first, declined); "Send N notes" sends (SIMULATED, nothing leaves Lumen), one note per patient in their language, 8 AM to 8 PM only. A send waits for a plan check that is still running. Status pills: Draft, Approved, Note sent, Sends 8 AM, Note out of date (after a re-plan moves the patient), Confirmed, Declined, No answer, Call.
* **Regions.** El Dorado County runs live against the API. Santa Clara County is an illustrative tour that runs in the page (`tour.js`, "Simulated outage"; chairs from `tour.capacity`). The tour calls AWS only for voice notes (`POST /speak`, demo key) and road routes (Amazon Location).
* A page reload restores the live state from `GET /state` and recomputes the routes.

Above 1180 px the rail and the plan panel resize: drag the line on their inner edge, or focus it and use the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps, Home and End for the limits); double-click resets. Limits: rail 280 to 480 px, panel 340 to 640 px, and the map keeps at least 420 px. Widths are saved in `localStorage` (`lumenRailW`, `lumenPanelW`). The rail, the panel and the review table scroll smoothly (instantly under reduced motion) and keep their place when they re-render. Below 1180 px wide, the plan panel is a drawer (Plan button). Below 760 px, the rail is a top sheet and the plan a bottom sheet.

The first visit follows the system light or dark setting; the theme toggle overrides it and is saved (`lumenTheme`).

## Configuration

`window.LUMEN_CONFIG` in `index.html`:

* `api`: the Lambda function URL. The contract is `contracts/api.md`.
* `mapKey`: the Amazon Location Service API key. The map uses Amazon Location Service (style Monochrome, us-east-2) with a referrer-restricted API key. The page falls back to OpenFreeMap tiles when the key or the style fails. The page also uses it for live road routes (`routes.geo.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/v2/routes`, needs `geo-routes:CalculateRoutes`); if those calls fail, the page shows dashed straight lines labeled "Road route not available".

The demo key is not in the repo. Open the key button in the top bar, paste the key and press Save. The page stores it in `localStorage` (`lumenDemoKey`) and sends it as `x-demo-key`. Ask the team for it.

## Data and labels

The PG&E notice (`PSPS-DEMO-01`) is simulated, and the page says so wherever it shows it. The feeders, their de-energized state and the Santa Clara shutoff area are illustrative. Transmission lines and substations come from OpenStreetMap. All patients are synthetic. The page labels each of these where it appears. Rules are demo assumptions, not clinical guidance.
