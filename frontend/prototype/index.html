Keep dialysis running when PG&E turns off the power.

PG&E warns about 48 hours before a Public Safety Power Shutoff. Lumen turns that warning into a plan: which centers lose power, which patients are affected, and where each one gets treated instead. Staff approve every move.

31 dialysis centers and 642 stations from CMS facility data. 2,568 synthetic patients. The PG&E notice and power grid are illustrative.
